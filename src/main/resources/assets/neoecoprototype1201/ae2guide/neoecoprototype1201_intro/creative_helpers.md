# Creative Helpers

Two tools that live only in the creative tab and ship no recipe. They exist so a tester can fill a
drive, a terminal or a whole crafting setup without hand-encoding hundreds of patterns.

## Creative All-Items Storage Matrix

Mounted in a drive, the <ItemLink id="neoecoprototype1201:all_items_creative_cell" /> lists every
item this addon and eco register and hands any of them out without limit. It never accepts a
deposit, so nothing in your other matrices is drawn away. It does not come apart either:
sneak-right-click refuses and dims the screen for ten seconds, exactly like the infinite concrete
matrix.

One caveat this line adds: here the matrix's listing is read as a very large *finite* amount, so
the network keeps paying power for whatever it draws. It is a creative convenience, not a free
machine.

## Pattern Generator

Right-click with the <ItemLink id="neoecoprototype1201:pattern_generator" /> to receive up to 9
encoded patterns per click: this addon's crafting and processor-assembly recipes, plus eco's two
working-station families. Each click skips the patterns you already carry, so repeated clicks
collect the full set. Recipes a pack adds under any other namespace are not handed out.
