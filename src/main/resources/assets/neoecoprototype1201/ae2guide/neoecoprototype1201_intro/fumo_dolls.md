# Fumo Dolls

Five plushies of people who helped this addon exist. They are decoration first: each one turns to face
whoever sets it down, lights its own cell, and keeps creepers six blocks away - the same answer vanilla
gives a cat, paid for by the doll rather than by every creeper in the world.

<Row>
  <BlockImage id="neoecoprototype1201:fumo_doll_yang120" scale="3"></BlockImage>
  <BlockImage id="neoecoprototype1201:fumo_doll_tedxenon" scale="3"></BlockImage>
</Row>

Wearing one on the head is not only cosmetic: every doll grants Night Vision (topped up while worn, so it
never blinks at the expiry boundary) and adds its own armour. The effects come from the same table the
tooltip quotes, so what the line says is what the game applies.

| Doll | Armour / Toughness | While worn | Where to get it |
|------|--------------------|------------|-----------------|
| <ItemLink id="neoecoprototype1201:fumo_doll_reliqwq" /> | 4 / 2 | Night Vision | L1 Processor Assembler: L1 storage controller + L1 computation system + L1 crafting system |
| <ItemLink id="neoecoprototype1201:fumo_doll_yang120" /> | 4 / 2 | Night Vision | L1 Processor Assembler: eco's L4 storage + L4 computation + L4 crafting hosts |
| <ItemLink id="neoecoprototype1201:fumo_doll_kouooki" /> | 1 / 5 | Night Vision | L1 Processor Assembler: pink dye + dried kelp + brown mushroom |
| <ItemLink id="neoecoprototype1201:fumo_doll_tedxenon" /> | 6 / 1 | Night Vision, Regeneration II, Luck, and it charges the items in your inventory (100 AE/t, the held slot included) | no recipe yet |
| <ItemLink id="neoecoprototype1201:fumo_doll_yang120_giant" /> | 4 / 2 | Night Vision | 9 plain Yang120 dolls in the crafting grid; one giant breaks back into 9 plain dolls |

Assembler recipes cannot be drawn on a page in this book, so the ingredients are in the table.

The giant plushie is one block, drawn three times the plain doll's size with her feet on the floor of the
cell she was placed in. She uses the plain doll's artwork rather than a copy of it, so a redraw shows up on
the shelf and on the giant in the same frame.

On this line the dolls are decoration only. The 1.21.1 line also ships Yang120's plushie as a crafting CPU,
which needs two mixins into AE2's crafting unit; that one is deliberately not ported here, and none of these
five blocks join a grid or act as a machine.
